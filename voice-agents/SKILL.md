# Voice Agents

Lessons from GAIA / *2147: A Voice from the Future*: a reclaimed payphone; lift the handset and GAIA, an AI speaking as the Earth from 2147, answers. No screens, no menus, no script.

## Principles

- **The first second matters.** Open with a short greeting; long intros lose people before the conversation starts. [PENDIENTE: confirm public figure before citing]
- **Character, not script.** Define the character with the ten [personality controls](../personality-controls/SKILL.md) and let the conversation be built in real time.
- **People ask its name.** At Sónar+D, GAIA was asked its name 289 times; only 9 of 505 full exchanges asked whether it was an AI. Design the name and the answer to "who are you?" first.
- **Design the sonic voice separately** from the verbal voice: test names, numbers and terms aloud.
- **Memory:** see [references/memory-mem0.md](references/memory-mem0.md).
